Travel-Sri-Lanka-website

Overview

This repository contains my project on a Travel Website using HTML, CSS, php, MY SQL and JavaScript. The purpose of our travel agency website is to effectively promote Sri Lanka's tourist destinations targeting local and foreign tourists. It can be introduced as a comprehensive road platform where tourists can discover the beauty, culture, accommodation, and attractions of Sri Lanka. We showcase various tourist destinations in Sri Lanka and we also focus on providing quality accommodation in high-quality hotels in Sri Lanka. Overall, the travel agency website serves as a powerful marketing tool to promote Sri Lanka as a desirable tourist destination. It not only highlights the country's beauty and attractions but also offers quality accommodation options and valuable resources to cater to the diverse needs of tourists, ultimately motivating them to choose Sri Lanka as their favorite travel destination.

Features

Introductory Page: Welcomes visitors with an engaging introductory page that highlights the essence of our travel website and sets the tone for exploration. Destination Page: Showcases top travel destinations with captivating imagery, detailed descriptions, and essential information to inspire and inform visitors. Hotel Packages: Presents the best hotel packages tailored to different interests and budgets, complete with itinerary details, pricing, and booking options for seamless travel planning. Contact Us Page: Provides a user-friendly contact page with essential contact information inquiry forms, and interactive maps for visitors to reach out and engage with our travel company effortlessly. Accommodation Booking: View and book accommodations in different regions.

Technologies Used

Front-End: HTML, CSS, JavaScript 
Back-End: PHP Database: MySQL
